## 1. One page that matters

Three to five sentences on the state of the system, written for the person who will not read further. Then the table.

| # | Finding | Severity | Owner | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | | Critical | | |

**What to do this week:** the one or two items that stop the bleeding.
**What to do this quarter:** the structural items.
**What is fine:** say what is good. It is true and it makes the rest credible.

## 2. Method

What was reviewed (repositories, environments, commits, dates), how (reading, tests, tooling), and what was out of scope. Findings are only as strong as this section.

## 3. Findings

### F-01 · [Title in the form of a fact] · Severity

- **Evidence:** file and line, query, request/response, screenshot. Reproducible.
- **Impact:** what happens, to whom, how much.
- **Likelihood:** how easily it is triggered (by load, by a routine attack, by a mistake).
- **Recommendation:** specific change, with an estimate of effort and any prerequisite.
- **Owner:** person, not team.
- **Status:** open | in progress | fixed on YYYY-MM-DD | accepted risk (signed by)

Repeat per finding, ordered by severity.

## 4. Observations

Things that are not findings: patterns worth keeping, decisions that deserve a written record, questions nobody could answer during the review.

## 5. Follow-up

Date of the read-through meeting, date of the re-check, and the list of items that will be verified as fixed.
